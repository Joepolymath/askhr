# Company Overview

Northwind Labs is a fictional company used for this demo. All content is sample data.

## What We Do

Northwind Labs is a Lagos-based digital insurer. We sell health, motor and gadget insurance through the Northwind app and a network of more than 1,200 independent agents, and we settle most claims within 48 hours. We were founded in 2019 and now cover more than 300,000 policyholders across Nigeria.

## Mission and Values

Our mission is to make insurance simple, affordable and trusted for every Nigerian household.

Our values are:

- **Customers first** — we pay valid claims quickly and explain decisions plainly.
- **Own it** — we take responsibility and follow through.
- **Clear over clever** — we communicate simply and honestly.
- **Better together** — we help each other and share credit.

## Offices

Our head office is in Victoria Island, Lagos. We also have an office in Wuse II, Abuja, and claims hubs in Port Harcourt and Kano. We have around 220 employees.

## Leadership and Teams

- **Executive** — led by the Chief Executive Officer, based at the Lagos head office.
- **Engineering** — builds the Northwind app, the claims dashboard and the underwriting system; led by the Chief Technology Officer.
- **Claims** — assesses and settles claims, including inspection visits to customers and repair workshops.
- **Underwriting** — prices policies and decides what we cover.
- **Finance** — payroll, expenses and financial reporting.
- **People & Culture** — HR policy, benefits, onboarding and employee experience.
