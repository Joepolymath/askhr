# Company Overview

Northwind Labs is a fictional company used for this demo. All content is sample data.

## What We Do

Northwind Labs builds route-optimisation and fleet-planning software for mid-sized freight and delivery companies. Our main product, Northwind Route, plans daily delivery routes, predicts arrival times and helps dispatchers react to delays in real time. We were founded in 2017 and serve more than 400 customers across North America and Europe.

## Mission and Values

Our mission is to make every delivery on time, with fewer miles driven.

Our values are:

- **Customers first** — we measure success by our customers' results.
- **Own it** — we take responsibility and follow through.
- **Clear over clever** — we communicate simply and honestly.
- **Better together** — we help each other and share credit.

## Offices

Our headquarters is in Austin, Texas. We also have an office in Lisbon, Portugal. We have around 180 employees.

## Working Hours and Hybrid Work

Northwind Labs works in a hybrid model. Employees who live within commuting distance of an office are expected in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time; outside those hours you may arrange your working day with your manager.
